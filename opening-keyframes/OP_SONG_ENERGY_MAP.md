## Hard constraint

**Minimum snippet length = 3s** (Kling / production floor).  
No 0.8–1.5s “flash only” gens. Short foreshadow beats must be **chained** (START panel A → END panel B) inside a ≥3s clip, or dropped/merged.

Rough budget **19→91 = 72s** → at most **24** snippets if everything is exactly 3s; in practice fewer, longer holds on hero beats.
