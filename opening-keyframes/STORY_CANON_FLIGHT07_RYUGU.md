# Beyond the Door — TRUE EVENT: Flight 07 / Ryugu Seal

**Status:** locked from Master’s brief.  
**Theme:** fate/chosen hearts; Digimon mythic scale; BZ earth-side only.

---

## Setup (human side)

Feris’s parents were on a **Zoodiac conglomerate business trip**.  
Their Bureau Zero **Project Mir** research had **already been revoked**.  
They were not sent by BZ into mystery — then the opening happened.

---

## Setup (Takamagahara)

In pressing times, a **ritual to call for aid from the human world** was used in Takamagahara.

- This fits older allusions: people have been **isekai’d / called across** long before **Digitokyo** existed.
- Conjured in emergency, the ritual reached for **someone who would help Digimon**.
- That is why it found **Feris’s parents** (pure-hearted Digimon-helpers / chosen by the call).

The ritual was performed **amidst a battle** between **Susanoomon** and **Magatsumon’s second-in-command — the General** (kept as a distinct late-arc villain weight; Magatsumon remains the deeper final threat).

---

## The taking of the plane

1. The ritual opens a **Digital Gate** onto the human flight path.  
2. The plane is drawn through toward Takamagahara.  
3. The Susanoomon vs General fight **breaks into / through** that space as well — battle in the skies around the gate and the aircraft.  
4. **Susanoomon protects the plane with his own body** to secure the humans inside.  
5. The humans **fall into the Digital World** (Takamagahara) with the ship.

---

## The seal (Tsukuyomon) → sky darkness → Ryugu

The battle **destabilized the Digital Gate**.

**Tsukuyomon** takes a hard decision: wrap **digital physics** around the gate and transform it into a **seal**, closing the **General** inside that wound.

### Mythic geography (locked)
- At that locus, the **skies of the human world and the skies of Takamagahara are the same sky**.  
- There hangs a **sphere of darkness** — the seal containing the General.  
- Releasing / entering it is dangerous and narratively charged.

### With time
- The black sphere **mutates into a palace** and **falls into the sea**.  
- It is called **Ryugu** (Japanese myth reference).

### Arc 4 entrance
- Best path for the children into the Digital World: **through that place (Ryugu / the old seal)**.  
- They must **confront the General** there — entrance challenge **and** a **moral decision** (break seal? bargain? imprison anew? risk Magatsumon’s hand waking?).

---

## Bureau Zero / Yamato (aftermath only)

- BZ did **not** cause the taking and did **not** enter Takamagahara.  
- Earth-side sensors / Digitokyo-facing systems catch horror-scale anomaly fallout.  
- **Yamato already feared Digimon before Flight 07** (prior incidents / doctrine forming). This event — the Mega-level display, Susanoomon/General sky war fallout — **worsens** that fear and hardens his behaviour.  
- That worsened fear explains his early-arc attitude toward **Feris and the other children/tamers**: containment, distrust, imprison-first instincts (as with proposing to capture Feris).  
- Manifest lockdown + Project Mir burial = covering a wound they cannot walk into.

---

## Parents afterward

In Takamagahara they try to go home, but cannot ignore Digimon/people pleading for help — they stay entangled in that duty until Arc 4’s reunion/confrontation.
