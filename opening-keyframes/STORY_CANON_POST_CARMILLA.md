# Beyond the Door — Story Canon (Post-Carmilla)

**Scope:** narrative canon only (not OP still locks unless noted).  
**Status:** locked from Master’s brief.

---

## 1. Feris collapse after Carmilla

After Carmilla is saved, the next day Feris goes to school in a sorry state: broken arm, sleepless, emotionally overwhelmed. She had a fever for about three days and ignored it.

At the school entrance she encounters **Yujiro**. After they talk, she passes out. She is taken to the infirmary, then relocated to the hospital. Feris sleeps through it all for **two days straight**.

---

## 2. While Feris sleeps

- **Yujiro ↔ Chernobog:** Chernobog explains what is happening to Feris. Yujiro passes that understanding on to the others.
- **Junko:** Promises to take care of Feris and make sure she will not hurt others again.
- **Vesper → Officer Makoto:** Goes to request Makoto’s help finding out more about Feris’s parents.
- **Yujiro → Tomura (when Tomura visits Feris):** Yujiro spins a whole tale that Bureau Zero took his father from him and killed him, and convinces Tomura to let him join **Red Thread**.

### Yujiro’s lie (truth)
His father was a deadbeat who left his mother. The Bureau Zero hatred is **made up from whole cloth** so Yujiro can be with Feris when she needs him most.

---

## 3. Feris wakes — Yujiro at her side

Feris wakes in the hospital with **Yujiro** beside her. They have a deep talk.

Yujiro explains that **Chernobog told him everything**.

Feris worries the others will be mad at her for not telling them. Yujiro dismisses that: they were **more worried than angry or disappointed**.

Feris did not mean to keep it from them — she simply did not know how to bring it up, and they did not pry either. She had been led to believe friends would just barge in and ask about her life; because they never did, she did not push it onto them, expecting them to ask first.

---

## 4. Makoto, Yamato, and the crack in Bureau Zero

**Timing note:** Makoto had **already** talked with **Yamato** (who visited her in the hospital) **before** Vesper’s visit the following day.

### Yamato ↔ Makoto (hospital)
They discuss Feris and her current state.

Yamato reveals that Bureau Zero / they had **nothing to do with Feris’s parents’ disappearance**, but anything he told Makoto in her current state would be doubted. They would need proof beyond a shadow of a doubt — which they **cannot obtain right now**.

Especially with Makoto on a warpath, she might threaten and hurt his subordinates while he is searching. His solution: try to **capture and imprison** Feris.

Makoto is left shaken by the idea they would imprison a **14-year-old** who needs help. Her mind wants to stay with Bureau Zero; her heart is already questioning what they are doing.

### Vesper ↔ Makoto (following day)
They have a deep talk about what happened.

Makoto reveals that she knows **Vesper’s name**. What she does **not** say: that **Vesper’s father works for them** (Bureau Zero).

Vesper requests Makoto’s **ID** so he can infiltrate Bureau Zero better and find information on Feris’s parents.

Makoto ultimately **agrees** to lend it to him, even though it will put her life at risk.
